# multi_scene_continuity | 多分镜连续

## familyId
`multi_scene_continuity`

## 支持 mediaType
- video

## 支持 storyPlan
- multi_cam

## 默认 sceneCount
3～6

## 默认 shot 范围
- mcu, ms, cu, fs, over_shoulder

## 默认 movement 范围
- static, slow_push_in

## 默认 cameraLanguage 范围
- dialogue_cover, cinematic_narrative

## 默认 lighting 范围
- time: day
- keyDir: top_left
- mood: warm
- lightingPack: natural_skin_readability

## 默认 composition 范围
- rule_of_thirds, eyeline_tension

## 默认 objects 类型
- character（含 continuityId）

## 允许 advancedTags
- continuity, multi_scene, advanced_camera, director_preset

## 允许 variants
- free_starter, basic_wide, basic_medium, basic_close
- vertical_9_16, horizontal_16_9, social_fast
- cinematic, multi_object, advanced_motion
