# product_compare | 双产品对比

## familyId
`product_compare`

## 支持 mediaType
- image

## 支持 storyPlan
- single

## 默认 sceneCount
1

## 默认 shot 范围
- mcu, fs, insert_closeup

## 默认 movement 范围
- static

## 默认 cameraLanguage 范围
- product_quality, commercial_ad

## 默认 lighting 范围
- time: day
- keyDir: top_left, top_right
- mood: bright
- lightingPack: ecommerce_white

## 默认 composition 范围
- left_right_standoff, rule_of_thirds, material_focus

## 默认 objects 类型
- product, prop, text（≥2 product）

## 允许 advancedTags
- advanced_camera, multi_object

## 允许 variants
- free_starter, basic_wide, basic_medium, basic_close
- vertical_9_16, horizontal_16_9, social_fast
- cinematic, multi_object, advanced_motion
